# Free / CC0 character & animation assets

Used for enemies whose original Tripo auto-rigs could not play death clips.

| File | Source | License | Notes |
|------|--------|---------|-------|
| `characters/_free_cc0/quaternius_wolf.glb` | Quaternius [Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html) (via [AnimaSim](https://github.com/danwahl/animasim) redistribute) | CC0 | Idle / Walk / Gallop / Attack / **Death** / HitReact |
| `characters/_free_cc0/oga_wild_boar.glb` | [OpenGameArt “Boar”](https://opengameart.org/content/boar) by Teh_Bucket (+ authored Idle/Death/Hit for Godot) | CC0 | Idle / Walk / Gallop / Attack / **Death** / HitReact — real wild-boar look |
| `characters/_free_cc0/gobkit_boar.glb` | [Gobkit Free Animal Pack Vol. 2](https://gobkit.com/api/free) | CC0 | Kept as spare; no longer spawned (looked too spiky / hedgehog-like) |
| `characters/_free_cc0/quaternius_bull.glb` | Quaternius Ultimate Animated Animals | CC0 | Kept as spare hoofed Death reference |

Original Tripo `wolf_enemy.glb` / `boar_enemy.glb` remain in the repo but are no longer spawned — their skeletons (`bone_0…`) cannot bind UAL or animal Death tracks.

Note: Quaternius Ultimate Animated Animals has **no boar** (wolf/fox/deer/etc. only). The Sketchfab “Animated Wild Boar” by AnimalMesh is personal-use only and cannot be shipped.
